# Module Breakdown

Modules below mirror the BRD scope and the BUC workflow. Each lists core functionality, key business rules (from the BUC/requirements), dependencies, and status.

**Status legend:** ⬜ Not Started · 🔶 In Progress · ✅ Complete

---

## 1. Auth & User Management  *(status: ⬜ Not Started — next to implement)*

**Core functionality**
- User CRUD, roles, active/inactive (ITAdmin/Admin).
- Login endpoint issuing JWTs; password hashing (BCrypt.Net-Next — already used in the source platform).
- Default-deny authorization + `[Authorize(Roles=...)]` policies; role mapping from claims.
- Audit log capture for sensitive actions.

**Key business rules**
- Roles: `Admin, Sales, Finance, Legal, PropertyManager, ITAdmin` (BRD User Management; stakeholder matrix).
- Only Admin/ITAdmin manage users; Legal-only actions are enforced at the endpoint/handler level.
- Every mutation record carries `createdBy` user (auditability NFR).

**Dependencies**
- `Infrastracture.Base.API` auth wrapper, middleware, authorization handlers (imported).

## 2. Property Management  *(status: ⬜ Not Started)*

**Core functionality**
- Property listings: type (villa, apartment, condo, commercial, land), location (sub-city/woreda/kebele), size m², rooms, amenities.
- Tenure tracking: leasehold (start/expiry, annual ground rent) vs freehold (rare).
- Attach Title Deed (የባለቤትነት ማረጋገጫ) and cadastral map; status lifecycle with history.
- Listing flyers in English and Amharic (Phase 1: PDF generation).

**Key business rules**
- Status values: `Available, UnderNegotiation, Reserved, Sold, Rented, LegalIssue, OnHold, OffMarket`.
- Negotiation-failure sets status back to `Available` with notes (BUC 3a); refusal to sign → `OnHold` (4a); encumbrance found → `LegalIssue` (6a) until external resolution.
- One active owner (seller/landlord) per property at a time.

**Dependencies**
- Client (owner), Document (title deed scans), Audit (status history).

---

## 3. Client Management  *(status: ⬜ Not Started)*

**Core functionality**
- Register clients: name, phone, email, ID type/number (passport, Kebele ID), TIN.
- Classify Buyer / Seller / Tenant / Landlord / Delala (a person may hold several roles).
- Interactions log (calls, meetings, site visits); preferences (budget, location, type); per-client transaction history.

**Key business rules**
- TIN captured for tax reporting (ERCA); mandatory for Finance-visible records.
- Delalas are **clients with role `Delala`**, not system users — they get commission statements, not login (stakeholder matrix).

**Dependencies**
- Sales/Finance read-only (transaction history), Document.

---

## 4. Sales Management  *(status: ⬜ Not Started)*

**Core functionality**
- Sales agreements with customizable templates; bilingual generation (Amharic/English).
- Auto-calculation of taxes & commissions (see rules).
- Installment payment schedules with due dates + reminders (Hangfire later).
- **Title-transfer workflow (UC-RE-001)**: verification → documents checklist → taxes paid → government transfer → new title deed upload.
- Commission records for agency and Delala.

**Key business rules** (from BUC UC-RE-001)
- Money in ETB; USD converted at daily NBE rate (captured at agreement time).
- Down payment ≥ 10% of sale price (recorded even if negotiated higher).
- **Capital gains tax 15%** on seller's profit — payable by Seller.
- **Stamp duty 2%** and **transfer tax 2%** — payable by Buyer, base = max(government-assessed value, agreed price).
- **Agency commission 2%**, **Delala commission 1%** (when involved).
- Legal Officer must verify title deed authenticity and absence of encumbrances **before** transfer; only Legal may set status `TitleTransferred`.
- Title-transfer checklist generated by system (step 7); deficiency notices on missing documents (7a); bounced payments alert Sales (8a); Delala disputes logged for management (10a).

**Dependencies**
- Property, Client, Document, Finance (payment/tax posting), Reporting.

---

## 5. Rental Management  *(status: ⬜ Not Started)*

**Core functionality**
- Lease agreements (typically 1-year renewable), rent due dates, late fees.
- Rent bills; partial payments + receipts; occupancy tracking.
- Maintenance requests (tenant → assign → resolve); eviction process with legal notice.

**Key business rules**
- Late payment handling configurable (fee % / grace); overdue flags (Risk R3 mitigation).
- Maintenance request lifecycle: `Reported → Assigned → InProgress → Resolved → Closed`.
- Lease statuses trackable to Rented/Sold on property.

**Dependencies**
- Property, Client, Finance (rent posting), Document (lease agreements, legal notices).

---

## 6. Finance & Accounting  *(status: ⬜ Not Started)*

**Core functionality**
- Payments: down payments, installments, rents, taxes, commissions (cash/bank transfer/cheque/e-payment); receipts.
- Multi-currency (ETB/USD) with daily exchange rates (manual entry or NBE import).
- General ledger: sales revenue, rental income, commission income, tax payable, bank accounts, cash; automatic posting from sales/rental.
- Bank reconciliation; bounced-cheque handling.
- ERCA tax reports (capital gains, stamp duty, transfer tax; VAT/withholding where applicable).

**Key business rules**
- Capital gains = 15% × (sale price − seller's cost basis) — **cost basis must be tracked** (open decision D-013).
- Taxes are configurable, not hard-coded (Risk R6: rate changes; changelog kept).
- Commission payable only after approval and completion (Risk R9; management approval before payment).

**Dependencies**
- Sales, Rental, Client, Document (receipts/vouchers).

---

## 7. Document Management  *(status: ⬜ Not Started — infrastructure ready)*

**Core functionality**
- Upload/download/delete scans & generated docs (title deeds, agreements, receipts, tax forms, commission vouchers).
- Metadata + integrity hash (sha256) in DB; bytes in MinIO via `IDocumentService`/`MinioDocumentService`.
- Deficiency notice tracking for missing documents (BUC 7a).

**Key business rules**
- Title-deed uploads restricted (Legal/Sales); deletions audited; full change + upload log with timestamps and user IDs (BUC NFR auditability).
- Documents attached polymorphically (property/agreement/client/title-transfer).

**Dependencies**
- MinIO (Infrastructure), PDF generation (iText + Amharic font).

---

## 8. Reporting & Analytics  *(status: ⬜ Not Started)*

**Core functionality**
- Monthly sales report (count, value, commissions), rental occupancy & collection, tax liability summary, P&L, Delala performance.
- Export to Excel/PDF; bilingual Amharic/English (BRD).

**Key business rules**
- Reports are read-only projections; heavy aggregations run async (Hangfire) or SQL-backed.
- ERCA exports in accepted formats (Excel for now — BRD integration constraint).

**Dependencies**
- All modules (read), Document/PDF, Exchange rates.

```
Document ────────────► Property, Sales, Rental, Finance
Property ◄───────────► Client
Sales ─► Finance (posting) ─► Reporting
Rental ─► Finance ─► Reporting
```

Status summary: **Auth (M4) is the first module to build**; Property/Client (M5) follow to establish the feature-folder pattern.
